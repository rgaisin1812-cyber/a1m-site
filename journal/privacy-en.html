Personal data policy

Updated 25 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The operator of a1m.am and the controller of visitors’ personal data is ООО «А1 Машинери» (A1 Machinery LLC), represented by its director, Renat Maratovich Gaisin. Postal address: Office 2, 16 Voskhod Street, Kazan, Russia. Email: info@a1machinery.ru.

When you contact us

To answer questions about machinery, parts or delivery, we use the name, phone number, email address or messaging username visible in the channel you choose, along with your message and any attachments you send. We receive and store the correspondence, retrieve the information relevant to your enquiry and reply using your contact details.

The enquiry page asks separately for your consent to process these details to answer your request. It then opens your email app, Telegram or WhatsApp. The company receives your message when you send it in that app. This consent applies to your enquiry.

Data used to operate the site

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. On the home pages, your browser requests a font from Google Fonts, sending your IP address and technical request details to that service. The site stores your language and cookie choices in your browser to remember them on future visits.

When Yandex Metrica runs

The site loads Yandex Metrica tag 114599279 only after you select “Allow”. It uses analytics cookies and technical data such as the page URL, visit time, referring site, device and browser type. We use the reports to count visits and understand how visitors find us. Session Replay is switched off.

You can use the site if you decline analytics. To change your choice, open “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page. Declining stops further collection by the tag in that browser.

Services involved

Your chosen email provider or messaging service handles messages you send through it. Following the Facebook group link opens an external service. These providers process data under their own policies and may use infrastructure outside your country.

Retention and deletion

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to answer and follow up on your request. If you enter into a contract, information needed to perform it or meet statutory recordkeeping obligations is kept on those grounds. Once the purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn, we stop processing and delete the data within the applicable statutory period unless another legal basis requires or permits retention.

Your rights and contact details

You can ask what personal data we process, request correction, restriction or deletion, or withdraw your consent. Write to info@a1machinery.ru or the postal address above. Include the contact details you used and the approximate date of your enquiry so we can locate it. You may also lodge a complaint with Roskomnadzor, the Russian data protection authority, or seek a remedy in court.
